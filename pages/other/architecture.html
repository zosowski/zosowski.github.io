Course Description

Architecture invites students to explore the built environment through design thinking. Learners analyze precedent projects, produce architectural drawings, and create 3D models using both physical and digital tools.

Topics include site analysis, spatial planning, building codes, and sustainability strategies. Students use industry-standard software to draft floor plans, elevations, and renderings.

Final projects present comprehensive design proposals for residential or community spaces, complete with client narratives and material boards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need drawing skills?

No formal art background is required. The course teaches sketching techniques and digital workflows from the ground up.

What software will we learn?

Students explore tools such as SketchUp, Revit, or AutoCAD, along with rendering platforms like Lumion.

Will we visit real buildings?

Yes. Local site visits and virtual tours expose students to diverse architectural styles and careers.
