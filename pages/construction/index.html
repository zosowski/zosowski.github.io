Construction Technology

Construction coursework focuses on residential and commercial building systems, emphasizing safety, project management, and sustainability. Students gain confidence through jobsite simulations and collaborative builds.

Learning Outcomes

  • OSHA-10 safety training and tool certification.
  • Blueprint reading, framing, electrical, and plumbing fundamentals.
  • Cost estimation, scheduling, and client communication skills.

Community Partnerships

Students partner with local trades to complete service projects, tour active jobsites, and prepare for apprenticeships. Advisory committees keep curriculum aligned with workforce needs.
