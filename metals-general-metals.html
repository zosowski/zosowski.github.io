Course Description

General Metals builds on foundational skills by introducing more complex fabrication processes and tighter tolerances. Students rotate through machining, forming, and welding stations while managing quality checkpoints for each project.

Team-based challenges simulate industry roles such as fabricator, inspector, and project manager, giving learners insight into manufacturing workflows. Emphasis is placed on reading prints, selecting the right tooling, and documenting adjustments.

Students finish the course with a portfolio of intermediate projects that highlight craftsmanship and problem-solving, preparing them for specialty courses such as Welding or Mass Production.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is General Metals different from Foundations of Metals?

General Metals moves faster, involves more independent work, and introduces additional machines that require proficiency with measurement and layout.

Will I earn any certifications?

While certifications are not awarded in this course, successful students are well prepared to pursue welding or machining credentials offered in advanced electives.

Can I repeat the class for credit?

Students may repeat General Metals with instructor approval if they are pursuing an advanced project or leadership role in the lab.
