Course Description

Aviation II builds on foundational knowledge with advanced navigation, flight planning, and weather analysis. Students complete cross-country flight simulations, interpret aviation charts, and study emergency procedures.

Guest pilots mentor students through scenario-based training and introduce career pathways in commercial aviation, unmanned systems, and aerospace engineering. Learners also explore aviation management and airport operations.

The course prepares students for private pilot ground school or aviation college programs, emphasizing leadership and decision-making.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we earn any certifications?

The course prepares students for FAA written exam content. Formal certification requires additional external training.

Is aviation math involved?

Yes. Students apply trigonometry and physics to calculate headings, fuel burn, and weight and balance considerations.

Can this class count toward science credit?

Credit alignment varies by year. Consult a counselor to confirm how Aviation II fits in your graduation plan.
