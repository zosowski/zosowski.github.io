Course Description

Sheet Metal focuses on layout, cutting, and forming light-gauge metals for both functional and decorative applications. Students learn pattern development, bend allowances, and fastening methods used in HVAC, aerospace, and product design.

Hands-on labs include shearing, punching, notching, and brake forming to create precise seams and joints. Learners integrate CAD files with CNC plasma cutters to accelerate production while maintaining tight tolerances.

Final projects encourage creativity as students design custom ductwork, enclosures, or sculptural pieces that highlight material efficiency and craftsmanship.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much math is involved?

Students apply measurement conversions, geometry, and trigonometry when developing patterns. Instruction includes step-by-step support to build confidence with calculations.

Can projects support other classes?

Yes. Sheet Metal students frequently collaborate with Engineering and Construction courses to fabricate custom components for shared projects.

Will I work with CAD software?

Students use CAD to model flat patterns and export cut files for CNC equipment, reinforcing digital fabrication skills.
