Course Description

AP Computer Science Principles offers a broad introduction to computing. Students investigate how computing impacts society, analyze data, and develop programs that solve real problems while preparing for the AP Performance Tasks and exam.

Coursework includes creative coding challenges, data visualization projects, and discussions about cybersecurity and the digital divide. Students build digital portfolios documenting their artifacts and reflections.

Successful completion can earn college credit and provides a strong foundation for AP Computer Science A or advanced programming electives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this class only for future programmers?

No. The course welcomes students interested in design, communication, and problem-solving across disciplines.

How much homework should I expect?

Expect regular reading, coding practice, and portfolio updates to stay on pace for the AP assessment.

Do we have to take the AP exam?

Students are strongly encouraged to take the exam to maximize college credit opportunities, but it is not mandatory.
