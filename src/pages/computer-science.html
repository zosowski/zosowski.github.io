Computer Science Pathways

Computer science courses introduce students to coding fundamentals, web and app development, and the ethical implications of computing. Emphasis is placed on creativity, collaboration, and iteration.

Courses Offered

  • Programming Fundamentals — Block-based and text-based programming in Python to build problem-solving skills.
  • Web & App Development — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript projects that culminate in mobile-friendly applications.
  • Cybersecurity & Networking — Explores security principles, ethical hacking, and infrastructure basics.

Student Opportunities

Learners join coding clubs, participate in hackathons, and collaborate with engineering teams on embedded systems. Internship partnerships provide exposure to software careers and agile workflows.
