Course Description

Engineering II: Applied Engineering empowers students to tackle open-ended problems in areas such as renewable energy, biomedical devices, and smart systems. Learners conduct research, analyze constraints, and prototype solutions using interdisciplinary tools.

Students collaborate with mentors from industry or higher education, gaining experience with project management software, user interviews, and technical documentation. Emphasis is placed on systems thinking and ethical decision making.

The course culminates with a formal design review where teams present their findings and reflect on how their solutions impact users and communities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are projects selected?

Students pitch project ideas, evaluate feasibility, and form teams around shared interests or community needs.

Will we work with real clients?

Yes. Applied Engineering frequently partners with nonprofits, school departments, or local businesses to ensure solutions are user-centered.

What presentation skills are developed?

Teams practice technical writing, poster design, and formal presentations to communicate engineering decisions clearly.
