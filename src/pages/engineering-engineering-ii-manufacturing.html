Course Description

Engineering II: Manufacturing blends engineering design with production techniques used in modern factories. Students analyze material properties, create process plans, and deploy CNC equipment to deliver precise components.

Emphasis is placed on fixture design, tolerancing, and automation as learners integrate robotics or PLC concepts into their workflows. Teams document quality metrics and apply lean principles to optimize throughput.

Community-based projects provide authentic experience producing parts for robotics teams, school facilities, or local businesses.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this class different from Mass Production?

Manufacturing emphasizes engineering analysis and automation, while Mass Production focuses on business operations and large-batch product delivery.

Do we work with industry partners?

Yes. Guest speakers and project briefs from local manufacturers expose students to real-world expectations.

What software tools are used?

Students leverage CAD/CAM software, statistical process control tools, and digital workflow platforms.
