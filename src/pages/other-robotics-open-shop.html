Course Description

Robotics Open Shop offers structured lab time for students to advance robotics projects beyond the traditional classroom setting. Teams prototype mechanisms, refine programming, and document engineering notebooks in preparation for competition season.

Mentors provide targeted workshops on advanced fabrication, sensor integration, and strategic analysis. Students set weekly goals, manage budgets, and maintain equipment readiness.

The flexible format supports competition teams, outreach initiatives, and cross-curricular collaborations with engineering and computer science courses.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is attendance tracked?

Students log hours and progress milestones each session to demonstrate growth and maintain eligibility.

Can new members join mid-term?

Yes, with instructor approval. Orientation sessions help new members learn safety protocols and team expectations.

Is transportation provided for events?

Competition travel is coordinated through the robotics team. Fundraising helps offset costs when possible.
