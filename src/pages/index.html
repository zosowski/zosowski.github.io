Welcome to STMA Technology & Engineering Education

The STMA Technology & Engineering Education department offers a collaborative learning environment where students experiment with tools, materials, and technologies to develop practical solutions. Our curriculum blends creativity with real-world skills, preparing students for career and college pathways.

Use the navigation to explore our specialized programs ranging from metals and woods to computer science and engineering. Each section highlights course pathways, featured projects, and ways to get involved.

Why Technology & Engineering Education Matters

Through authentic problem-solving, students build confidence, strengthen teamwork, and practice resilience. Whether they are crafting a piece of furniture, programming an application, or fabricating a custom part, they develop a mindset that translates beyond the classroom.
