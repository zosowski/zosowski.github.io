Course Description

Woods I introduces students to woodworking safety, measurement, and core joinery techniques. Learners operate saws, planers, sanders, and handheld tools with instructor guidance before applying their skills independently.

Projects emphasize accuracy and craftsmanship, ranging from cutting boards to small furniture items. Students reflect on progress in a design journal that documents measurements, material selection, and finishing choices.

The course builds a solid foundation for advanced woodworking experiences, empowering learners to confidently take on more complex builds in Woods II.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear to class?

Closed-toe shoes and clothing that can get dusty are required. Long hair must be tied back and jewelry removed before lab work.

Do I need to purchase lumber?

Standard project materials are supplied. Students may purchase specialty hardwoods if they choose to customize projects.

How much homework should I expect?

Most work is completed during class. Short reflections and project planning sketches may be assigned outside of lab time.
