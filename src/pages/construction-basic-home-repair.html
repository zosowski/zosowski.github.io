Course Description

Basic Home Repair equips students with practical skills to maintain and improve residential spaces. Learners practice drywall patching, plumbing repairs, electrical troubleshooting, and preventative maintenance routines.

Projects are built around real-life scenarios, giving students confidence to diagnose issues, source materials, and complete repairs safely. Emphasis is placed on tool selection, safety, and clear communication with homeowners.

The course is ideal for students interested in DIY projects, future homeownership, or introductory exposure to trades careers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we work on real houses?

Most work occurs in lab modules that simulate residential systems. Select projects may involve on-campus maintenance opportunities.

What safety gear is required?

Safety glasses and closed-toe shoes are required daily. Gloves, hearing protection, and masks are provided as needed.

Will this course help with future trades programs?

Yes. Students gain foundational skills and confidence that transfer to advanced construction courses and apprenticeship pathways.
