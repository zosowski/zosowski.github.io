Metals & Manufacturing

Students explore fabrication fundamentals through welding, machining, and computer-aided manufacturing. Safety and precision are emphasized at every stage as students move from concept sketches to finished products.

Program Highlights

  • MIG, TIG, and stick welding certifications with dedicated booth stations.
  • Manual and CNC machining for milling, turning, and plasma cutting.
  • Industry-standard layout, measuring, and inspection techniques.

Capstone Experiences

Advanced students take on custom fabrication projects for community partners, compete in SkillsUSA manufacturing events, and learn to maintain shop equipment. Portfolio-ready documentation supports apprenticeships and technical college admissions.
