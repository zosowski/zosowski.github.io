Other Technology & Engineering Classes

Beyond our core pathways, we offer complementary courses that broaden student experiences and build transferable skills. These classes connect technology concepts with art, business, and everyday problem-solving.

Electives to Explore

  • Design & Entrepreneurship — Combines product design with marketing to launch a mini venture.
  • Digital Fabrication Lab — 3D printing, laser cutting, and vinyl cutting for rapid prototyping.
  • Home Maintenance — Teaches practical skills such as wiring, drywall repair, and fixture installation.

Plan Your Journey

Students meet with counselors to map multi-year plans that stack skills, earn industry certifications, and qualify for articulated college credit. Clubs and summer workshops keep momentum going all year.

Electronics

Investigate circuits, components, and testing equipment while building functional prototypes and troubleshooting systems.

  • Grades: 9-12
  • Length: 1 trimester

Fishing

Study aquatic ecosystems, tackle selection, and conservation practices alongside hands-on angling experiences.

  • Grades: 9-12
  • Length: 1 trimester

Small Engines

Disassemble and rebuild engines to understand combustion principles, diagnostics, and preventative maintenance.

  • Grades: 9-12
  • Length: 1 trimester

Aviation I

Discover aviation history, flight theory, and simulator practice while exploring careers in aerospace.

  • Grades: 10-12
  • Length: 1 trimester

Aviation II

Dive deeper into navigation, weather analysis, and flight planning while advancing simulator proficiency.

  • Grades: 11-12
  • Length: 1 trimester

Architecture

Blend artistic vision with technical drawing as you design structures using both hand sketching and CAD tools.

  • Grades: 10-12
  • Length: 1 trimester

Robotics Open Shop

Collaborate with peers to iterate on competition robots, practice advanced fabrication, and mentor younger teams.

  • Grades: 9-12
  • Length: 1 trimester
