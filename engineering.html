Engineering

Engineering courses challenge students to identify real-world problems and develop tested solutions. Learners blend CAD modeling, prototyping, and data analysis while refining communication skills.

Course Progression

  1. Introduction to Engineering Design — Foundational course featuring sketching, CAD, and design briefs.
  2. Principles of Engineering — Studies mechanical systems, energy, and control through lab experiments.
  3. Engineering Development & Design — Senior-level capstone partnering with mentors for open-ended projects.

Beyond the Classroom

Students compete on robotics teams, collaborate with math and science departments, and attend STEM career nights. Advisory boards keep curriculum aligned with industry expectations.
