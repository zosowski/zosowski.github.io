Woods

The woods program cultivates craftsmanship through project-based learning that spans furniture design, cabinetry, and architectural models. Students master both traditional tools and computer-controlled equipment.

Skills Students Develop

  • Safe operation of jointers, planers, table saws, and routers.
  • Precision measuring, joinery techniques, and finishing processes.
  • Computer-aided design and CNC routing for custom components.

Showcase & Community Impact

Learners present final pieces at the annual design showcase and partner with local organizations to build custom installations. Many graduates pursue careers in architecture, industrial design, or carpentry apprenticeships.

Woods I

Gain foundational woodworking skills with an emphasis on accurate measurement, safe tool operation, and finishing basics.

  • 9-12
  • 1 trimester

Woods II: Cabinetmaking

Design and build custom cabinetry while mastering case construction, hardware installation, and advanced joinery.

  • 10-12
  • 1 trimester

Woods II: Furniture Making

Explore furniture design principles and produce heirloom-quality pieces using complex joinery and finishing techniques.

  • 10-12
  • 1 trimester

Woods III

Lead independent projects that integrate CNC production, specialty tooling, and client collaboration experiences.

  • 11-12
  • 1 trimester
